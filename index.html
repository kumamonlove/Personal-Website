I am a Robot Learning Engineer at Humanoid Robot (Shanghai) Co., Ltd., focusing on embodied intelligence and robot learning. My interests center on combining reinforcement learning, imitation learning, and model predictive control to help robots learn from observations and interactions in the real world.

Previously, I studied Technology Innovation – Robotics at the University of Washington and conducted research in the Robot Learning Lab @ UW. There, I explored the connection between learning, planning, and control on real robots.

Before joining UW, I received my Bachelor's degree with High Honors from the College of Computer Science and Technology at Zhejiang University, along with a minor in Business Administration.
